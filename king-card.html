THE IDEA

The king
was always you.

We keep searching for a winning card: the right tool, the right opportunity, someone else’s permission.

King Card turns that search into a fantastical pursuit. As the magician moves through shifting worlds, the quest for an external source of power becomes a discovery of his own agency.

The magic carries an allegory about creation, work and the choices that shape a life. No card can decide who you are. The decisive move is recognizing that the ability to act—to imagine, choose and create—was yours all along.

Kling gives the impossible a visible form. The person holding the cards gives it direction.
