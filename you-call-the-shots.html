PRODUCT FILM / CAPABILITY BREAKDOWN

You direct.
The model follows.

A demonstration of reference control, motion, performance and visual quality.

  • Omni Reference

    Combine image, video and audio references to guide a scene and maintain visual consistency. The launch film’s description specifies up to 15 multimodal references.

  • Multiple Keyframes

    Direct how a sequence develops through multiple visual checkpoints. The official description lists control with up to 10 keyframes.

  • Dynamic Motion & Action

    Fast movement and fight choreography put motion clarity, physical interaction and action staging at the center of the demonstration.

  • Slow Motion

    Stretch a moment to emphasize gesture, movement and impact, giving action a different pace and dramatic weight.

  • Multilingual Performance

    Dialogue across languages, accents and dialects, supported by improved lip sync and stereo audio.

  • Longer, Connected Sequences

    Native generation up to 30 seconds, plus video extension, gives a scene more room to develop.

  • 4K & 10-bit HDR

    The launch showcase highlights higher-resolution output and a wider tonal range for cinematic imagery.
